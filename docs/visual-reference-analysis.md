# Visual Reference Analysis

What each reference does well, and how Classroom Creatures adapts the principle without copying any design, character, name, sound or brand.

| Reference | Principle we adopt | How it is adapted here |
|---|---|---|
| **Classroom point systems (ClassDojo-style)** | One tap from a student to a positive award; avatars on a grid; whole-class awards; projector mode | Card grid with three pinned quick-award buttons per card; tap card → profile with full award grid; multi-select bar; whole-class award; a Present mode with first names only. Unlike typical point trackers there is no public points total or ranking anywhere students can see. |
| **Creature-collecting RPGs (Pokédex-style)** | Strong silhouettes, elemental families, a dramatic evolution reveal, a collection index | 30 original species in 10 elements, each with a distinct ear/tail/wing/face silhouette. Evolution sequence: dim → glow to white silhouette → energy gathers → flash → spring reveal with rays and confetti → "YOUR CREATURE EVOLVED!". A Creature Collection page shows every species and all five forms. |
| **Monster-tamer variety (Temtem-style)** | Many distinct species identities, clear type labels | Every species has a personality line, a named ability, a legendary title, a rarity, and its own idle style and synthesised voice. Element badges use custom glyphs, not emoji. |
| **Language-learning rewards (Duolingo-style)** | Instant, juicy feedback; achievements; streak-like goals; expressive characters | Floating "+5 XP" with chime and creature squash-and-stretch on every award; level-up and achievement banners; daily/weekly challenges; creatures change expression (happy, excited, wow, sleepy). |
| **Open-world creature games (Palworld-style)** | Creatures feel alive in a world; personality through motion | Each creature lives in an element habitat (sunset for fire, underwater bubbles for water, starfield for cosmic…), with unlockable backgrounds. Tapping a creature cycles jump → wave → ability burst → happy spin with its own voice. |

## Things deliberately *not* adopted
- No public leaderboards, no "worst student" views, no point-loss shaming. Sorting options are name, creature and recent activity, never XP.
- No losing a creature, no de-evolution.
- No randomised loot boxes: rarity is assigned with the creature and is purely cosmetic.

## Resulting identity
A twilight fantasy world (deep indigo sky with soft aurora glows) holding bright parchment cards and colourful habitats, rounded friendly type (Fredoka + Nunito), chunky gold primary buttons, and chibi-proportioned creatures with thick soft outlines and glossy highlights.
